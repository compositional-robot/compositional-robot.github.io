About

Compositional learning builds complex behavior from reusable parts: skills, sensing modalities, constraints and models. Large models generalize well within the data they have seen, but the combinations a robot meets in the real world grow faster than any dataset. Composing parts at test time, across time (chaining skills), across modalities (vision, touch, force, language) and across concepts (goals, objects, constraints), is one way to cover them.

This page collects workshops and papers on the topic. It is maintained in the open, and anyone can add to it.
